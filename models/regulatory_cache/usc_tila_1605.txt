
## 15 U.S. Code § 1605 - Determination of finance charge

[15 U.S. Code § 1605 - Determination of finance charge] U.S. Code
[15 U.S. Code § 1605 - Determination of finance charge] Notes
[15 U.S. Code § 1605 - Determination of finance charge] Authorities (CFR)
[15 U.S. Code § 1605 - Determination of finance charge] Charges or premiums for insurance, written in connection with any consumer credit transaction, against loss of or damage to property or against liability arising out of the ownership or use of property, shall be included in the finance charge unless a clear and specific statement in writing is furnished by the creditor to the person to whom the credit is extended, setting forth the cost of the insurance if obtained from or through the creditor, and stating that the person to whom the credit is extended may choose the person through which the insurance is to be obtained.
