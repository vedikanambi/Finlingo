
## § 9-609. SECURED PARTY’S RIGHT TO TAKE POSSESSION AFTER DEFAULT.

[§ 9-609. SECURED PARTY’S RIGHT TO TAKE POSSESSION AFTER DEFAULT.] (a) [Possession; rendering equipment unusable; disposition on debtor's premises.]
[§ 9-609. SECURED PARTY’S RIGHT TO TAKE POSSESSION AFTER DEFAULT.] After default, a secured party :
[§ 9-609. SECURED PARTY’S RIGHT TO TAKE POSSESSION AFTER DEFAULT.] (1) may take possession of the collateral; and
[§ 9-609. SECURED PARTY’S RIGHT TO TAKE POSSESSION AFTER DEFAULT.] (2) without removal, may render equipment unusable and dispose of collateral on a debtor 's premises under Section 9-610 .
[§ 9-609. SECURED PARTY’S RIGHT TO TAKE POSSESSION AFTER DEFAULT.] (b) [Judicial and nonjudicial process.]
[§ 9-609. SECURED PARTY’S RIGHT TO TAKE POSSESSION AFTER DEFAULT.] A secured party may proceed under subsection (a):
[§ 9-609. SECURED PARTY’S RIGHT TO TAKE POSSESSION AFTER DEFAULT.] (1) pursuant to judicial process; or
[§ 9-609. SECURED PARTY’S RIGHT TO TAKE POSSESSION AFTER DEFAULT.] (2) without judicial process, if it proceeds without breach of the peace.
[§ 9-609. SECURED PARTY’S RIGHT TO TAKE POSSESSION AFTER DEFAULT.] (c) [Assembly of collateral.]
[§ 9-609. SECURED PARTY’S RIGHT TO TAKE POSSESSION AFTER DEFAULT.] If so agreed, and in any event after default, a secured party may require the debtor to assemble the collateral and make it available to the secured party at a place to be designated by the secured party which is reasonably convenient to both parties.
