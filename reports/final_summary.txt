FinLingo++ - results summary

FinLingo++ takes a financial/legal contract, breaks it into clauses, rewrites
each one in plain English, tags a risk category, and checks the rewrite
against real regulatory text to see if it's faithful. The thesis report and
configuration manual cover the system and the experiments in full.

Benchmark: 91 clauses / 182 rows, drawn from CUAD and ContractNLI.

Simplification quality (RQ1): SARI 54.36, BERTScore 0.898, FK grade 6.7-8.9.
All three clear their targets (SARI > 40, BERTScore > 0.88, FK grade <= 9.0).

Risk classification (RQ2): macro-F1 0.79 against a target of 0.75, from a
fine-tuned classifier (legal-bert + LoRA). This beats the prompted baseline
(0.56) and a frozen-embedding baseline (0.69).

Faithfulness verification (RQ3): precision 0.32 and recall between 0 and 1
depending on setup, against targets of 0.90 and 0.80. This does not clear
the bar. Most of the benchmark's clauses are commercial contract terms with
no matching regulatory citation to find, and the verifier itself doesn't
discriminate well on long regulatory text. A calibration attempt and a LoRA
fine-tune were both tried on real labelled data and both fell short - a
genuine negative result, not a bug. The full root-cause analysis is in
reports/proposal_gap_analysis.md.

The deployed simplifier and risk classifier run on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct
rather than Mistral-7B, for GPU memory reasons (documented in
reports/submission_manifest.json).

Full numbers and write-up: reports/final_results.json (authoritative per-RQ
numbers), reports/ablations.json (ablation study), reports/proposal_gap_analysis.md
(what was tried and why), reports/methodology_and_limitations.md.
