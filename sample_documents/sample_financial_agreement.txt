CONSUMER FINANCIAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Meridian Consumer Finance LLC
("Provider") and the undersigned account holder ("Customer"), effective as of the date
of account opening.

1. ACCOUNT SERVICES

Provider shall maintain a revolving credit account for Customer's use in accordance with
the terms set forth herein. Customer may access account services through the mobile
application, website, or by contacting customer support during business hours.

2. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL

This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless
Customer provides written notice of cancellation at least thirty (30) days prior to the
end of the then-current term. Provider will send a renewal reminder notice to the email
address on file no later than forty-five (45) days before the renewal date. If Customer
does not cancel, the subscription fee will be charged automatically to the payment
method on file.

3. FEES AND CHARGES

Customer agrees to pay a monthly maintenance fee of $9.99, billed on the first day of
each billing cycle. In addition to the monthly maintenance fee, the following charges
may apply: a late payment fee of $35.00 if a payment is not received within five (5)
days of the due date; a returned payment fee of $25.00 for any payment returned unpaid
by Customer's financial institution; and a foreign transaction fee equal to 3% of the
transaction amount for purchases made outside the United States. Provider reserves the
right to modify these fees upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Provider's total liability arising
out of or related to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort, or otherwise, shall not
exceed the total fees paid by Customer to Provider in the twelve (12) months preceding
the event giving rise to the claim. In no event shall Provider be liable for indirect,
incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, including loss of profits or
data, even if advised of the possibility of such damages. Customer agrees to indemnify
and hold Provider harmless from any claims arising from Customer's breach of this
Agreement.

5. DATA COLLECTION AND SHARING

Provider collects Customer's personal and financial information, including transaction
history, account balances, and device identifiers, in order to provide and improve its
services. Provider may share this information with affiliated companies, marketing
partners, and third-party service providers for purposes including credit risk
assessment, fraud prevention, and targeted advertising. Customer's data may be
transferred to and processed in jurisdictions outside of the Customer's country of
residence. Customer may opt out of marketing-related data sharing by contacting customer
support, but data sharing necessary for account servicing and regulatory compliance
cannot be opted out of.

6. LATE PAYMENT AND DEFAULT

If Customer fails to make a scheduled payment within thirty (30) days of its due date,
Customer shall be in default under this Agreement. Upon default, Provider may charge a
penalty interest rate of 29.99% APR on the outstanding balance, accelerate all amounts
owed under this Agreement, and report the default to consumer credit reporting agencies.
Provider may also assign or refer the delinquent account to a third-party collection
agency.

7. CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Provider will make commercially reasonable efforts to respond to Customer support
inquiries within two (2) business days. Customer support is available via phone, email,
and in-app chat during Provider's normal business hours, Monday through Friday,
9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Time, excluding federal holidays.

8. ACCOUNT STATEMENTS

Provider will make monthly account statements available electronically through the
mobile application and customer web portal. Customer may request a paper statement at
no additional charge by contacting customer support.

9. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the
State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement, together with any amendments issued by Provider from time to time,
constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to its subject matter
and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings, whether written or oral.
